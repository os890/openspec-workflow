# Proposal

## Why

A habit sticks when you see it holding. A streak, the number of days in a row a habit was done, is the simplest
motivation the app can give.

## What Changes

- Habits with a name; one check-in per habit and day, undoable on the same day.
- Current streak and longest streak per habit, computed from the check-ins.

Out of scope: reminders (`add-reminders`), statistics beyond streaks (`add-statistics`).

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `habits/streaks`: daily check-ins and streaks.
