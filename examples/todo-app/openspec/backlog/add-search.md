# Proposal

## Why

With more than a screen of todos, users need to find one quickly by a word or show only open or done ones.

## What Changes

- Text filter over titles, case-insensitive, as you type.
- Status filter: open, done, all; remembered per browser.

Out of scope: full-text search over notes, saved searches.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `tasks/search`: filter todos by text and status.
