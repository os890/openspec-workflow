# Proposal

## Why

Due dates only help if users look at the list. A reminder e-mail the day before brings due todos to them.

## What Changes

- Daily job sends one e-mail per user listing open todos due tomorrow.
- Users can switch reminders off in their settings.

Out of scope: push notifications, reminders at a chosen time.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `notifications/reminders`: e-mail reminders for todos due tomorrow.
