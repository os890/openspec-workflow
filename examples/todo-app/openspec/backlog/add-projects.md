# Proposal

## Why

Todos pile up in one long list. Projects let users group related todos ("Home", "Work") and focus on one group at a time.

## What Changes

- A project has a name (1–60 characters) and a colour.
- A todo belongs to at most one project; the list can be filtered by project.
- Deleting a project keeps its todos without a project.

Out of scope: sharing projects (`add-sharing`), nested projects.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `organisation/projects`: group todos into projects.
